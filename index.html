01 — Why It Matters

The gains are real, and you can count them.

Whether AI helps is no longer the question. Studies and national surveys now put numbers on it. Teams finish more work each hour. Staff get hours back every week. Income grows enough to show up on the books. Workers who use AI save about 5% of their work time — roughly two hours a week each. And the businesses winning here are not the ones with the biggest budgets. They are the ones that build AI into the work instead of setting it beside the work.

15%more work finished per hour by teams using AI
34%gain for the newest staff — AI helps beginners most
70%of small businesses say AI raised their income last year
73%say it helped them keep up with bigger companies

Where these numbers come from: Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond, “Generative AI at Work,” Quarterly Journal of Economics (2025); Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, The Impact of Generative AI on Work Productivity; U.S. Chamber of Commerce, Empowering Small Business; Thryv AI & Small Business Adoption Survey (2026).

Five people who each get five hours back add up to a sixth staff member — with no sixth paycheck.

02 — The Gap

Buying AI is easy. Getting paid back is not.

Almost every business has started using AI. Most have nothing to show for it. The tool is rarely the problem. Someone bought it, handed it out, and never built it into real work — no owner, no plan, and no way to tell if it helped.

95%of company AI test projects show no gain you can measure
80%of companies see no real change in what they earn
5%of AI projects capture almost all of the value

Where these numbers come from: MIT Project NANDA, The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business (2025); McKinsey, The State of AI.

What makes the 5% different is not the software. It is everything built around it — better work steps, one person in charge, staff training, and clear written rules.

09 — What You Get Back

Do the math before you spend a dollar.

A readiness check is a set fee of a few thousand dollars, one time. One more full-time hire costs many times that, every single year. A new hire also adds help in a straight line. But a work system that saves four hours a week for five people adds help every week it runs. It needs no training, and it does not quit in October.

The studies agree on where the payback really comes from. Not a smarter tool, but the same people finishing more work each hour — with the biggest gains going to your newest staff. If you rebuild half your team every season, that is the finding that matters most.

So we do not sell you with big words. We measure what a task costs you today, build against it, then measure again. If the number does not move, we tell you to stop.

Help you build keeps growing. Help you hire has to be hired again next year.
